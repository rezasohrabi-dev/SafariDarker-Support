Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Darker · Last updated: October 19, 2026

Darker ("we", "us" or "our") is a Safari extension and app for Mac, iPhone and iPad published by Mohammad Reza Sohrabi. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and what we do not do with it.

Information Collection and Use

Darker collects nothing about you. We operate no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app handles the following data on your device only:

  • Websites you visit: the Safari extension needs permission to access websites so it can change how they look on your device. It never reads, stores or sends the content of the pages you visit, and it never records which sites you visit.
  • Settings: whether dark mode is on, the darkness level, and the sites you switched on or off. Stored on your device by Safari.
  • Subscription state: whether your subscription is active and when it renews. The app reads this from the App Store and shares it with its own Safari extension on the same device. None of it is visible to us.

We do not collect email addresses, names, device identifiers, location, browsing history or usage data. No data is sold, shared or transferred to us or to any third party.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third-Party Services

Darker uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

Darker does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
