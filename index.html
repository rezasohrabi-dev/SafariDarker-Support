Darker for Safari · Support

Dark mode for every website in Safari, on Mac, iPhone and iPad

Darker turns light websites into a calm, warm dark mode while photos and videos keep their real colours. A Darkness slider goes from soft warm gray to pure black, and you can switch it on or off for each site.

Need help? Email sohrabi.developer@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your macOS or iOS version and the address of the website that does not look right.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on the extension?

Mac: open Safari, choose Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Extensions, tick Darker and allow it on all websites.
iPhone and iPad: open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions ▸ Darker, turn it on and set All Websites to Allow.

Pages are not turning dark

  • Open the Darker app and check that Darker is unlocked or your free trial is active.
  • Click the Darker button in Safari's toolbar (on iPhone, in the page menu of the address bar) and check that Dark Mode and This site are both on.
  • Sites that already have a dark design are recognised and left as they are on purpose.
  • Reload the page after changing a setting.

How do I change how dark pages are?

Use the Darkness slider in the Darker toolbar menu. Pages change while you drag.

How much does it cost?

Darker is free to download. Dark mode needs a one-time purchase: Darker Lifetime, $4.99 (prices vary by country). Pay once and keep it forever, with no subscription. You can try it free for 3 days first; the trial never turns into a purchase by itself. One purchase works on all your Mac, iPhone and iPad devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the Darker app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account that bought it.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
