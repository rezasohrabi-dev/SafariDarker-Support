SafariDarker Support

Dark mode for every website in Safari, on Mac, iPhone and iPad

SafariDarker turns light websites into a calm, warm dark mode while photos and videos keep their real colours. A Darkness slider goes from soft warm gray to pure black, and you can switch it on or off for each site.

Need help? Email sohrabi.developer@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your macOS or iOS version and the address of the website that does not look right.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on the extension?

Mac: open Safari, choose Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Extensions, tick SafariDarker and allow it on all websites.
iPhone and iPad: open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions ▸ SafariDarker, turn it on and set All Websites to Allow.

Pages are not turning dark

  • Open the SafariDarker app and check that your subscription or free trial is active.
  • Click the SafariDarker button in Safari's toolbar (on iPhone, in the page menu of the address bar) and check that Dark Mode and This site are both on.
  • Sites that already have a dark design are recognised and left as they are on purpose.
  • Reload the page after changing a setting.

How do I change how dark pages are?

Use the Darkness slider in the SafariDarker toolbar menu. Pages change while you drag.

How much does it cost?

SafariDarker is free to download. Dark mode needs a subscription: .99 per year or /bin/zsh.99 per month (prices vary by country). New subscribers get a 3-day free trial, which starts when you subscribe. One subscription works on all your Mac, iPhone and iPad devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or App Store ▸ [your name] ▸ Account Settings ▸ Subscriptions on Mac. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. The app's Manage Subscription button opens the same page.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the SafariDarker app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account that subscribed.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
